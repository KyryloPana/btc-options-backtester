# Execution timing and historical settlement policy (Task 4)

Policy version `execution-timing-v2` uses four deliberately separate clocks. The **signal** is the thesis timestamp; the **order-available** timestamp follows the decision/latency rule. An immediate scenario then works only inside its bounded 30, 60, or 120 minute fill-search horizon. Each leg accumulates qualifying causal tape independently; spread completion is the later leg completion, and completion delay is spread completion minus order availability. “Immediate” therefore means submitted without a configured delayed-entry bucket, not instantaneous.

The old exported `synchronizationThresholds.milliseconds = 60000` described the legacy one-minute default of `simulateTakerSpread`; it did not describe the research engine that produced the exported rows. Research entry execution already passed a one-hour leg synchronization gate, so the old metadata was misleading rather than a valid 60-second quality boundary. It is replaced by named millisecond fields.

The final policy keeps a one-hour hard leg gap for both immediate and delayed observed reconstruction. A gap up to 30 minutes is Green; greater than 30 and up to 60 minutes is Yellow; beyond one hour is Red/unavailable and cannot create an observed entry. The historical two-hour language remains diagnostic only: a two-hour fill-search horizon may locate evidence, but evidence found in that horizon must still form a pair inside the independent one-hour gate. Task 2's predetermined 0–2h through next-trading-day ladder remains causal and unchanged apart from its policy-version bump and persisted legging diagnostics.

Official settlement is accepted only when both exact contracts share the selected expiry and the stored `btc_usd` delivery record has the matching UTC expiry date and `deribit-get_delivery_prices` source. `inverse-settlement-v2` reuses canonical inverse intrinsic and versioned delivery-fee accounting. The opening BTC cash flow is converted at the entry index and terminal BTC cash flow at official delivery price for analytical USD PnL. Missing or mismatched official data remains explicitly unavailable. Compact settlement provenance is stored with the immutable selected candidate so refresh/export does not depend on a later API call.

Derived recomputation order is contracts and persisted delivery provenance, immediate execution, reference path and settlement outcomes, delayed execution, then modeled execution. Modeled outcomes are rebuilt from the settlement-enriched reference snapshot by the existing modeled-opening-ledger adjustment path. Margin remains outside this work; `openingMarginBtc` in delayed diagnostics is currently a maximum-economic-loss alias and must not be treated as verified historical margin.
